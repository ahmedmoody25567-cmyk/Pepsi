History of Pepsi

Pepsi began in 1893 in New Bern, North Carolina, when pharmacist Caleb Bradham mixed a drink he called "Brad's Drink". In 1898 he renamed it Pepsi-Cola.

Pepsi around the world

Today Pepsi is sold in more than 200 countries and territories, with the same blue, red and white globe recognised on every continent.
